Zaiyi Zheng

Ph.D. Student, Department of Computer Engineering, University of Virginia

University of Virginia

Charlottesville, VA

I am currently a Ph.D. Candidate in the program of Computer Engineering at the University of Virginia, advised by Professor Jundong Li. I started my Ph.D. in Fall 2023. Before that, I received my B.E. in Computer Science from the University of Science and Technology of China. My research interests lie in building efficient and effective foundational models for the retrieval system.
